# Filled Example: Weekly Workshop Notes

This fictional example shows the level of detail to aim for. It contains no real client or account information.

## Memory index

- **Scope:** A weekly public workshop about home gardening.
- **Read first:** `PROJECT STATE.md`
- **Slow-changing context:** `CONTEXT.md`
- **Past choices:** `DECISIONS.md`
- **Proof of completed work:** `RECEIPTS.md`
- **Source of truth:** The approved workshop outline in the working folder.
- **Never store:** Attendee contact details, payment records, login details, or private questions.

## Context

- **Purpose:** Publish one clear workshop outline each Friday.
- **Desired outcome:** A beginner can follow the outline without extra explanation.
- **Working rule:** Use plain language and define gardening terms.
- **Quality standard:** One topic, three teaching points, one safe exercise, and a final review by the workshop owner.
- **Stable fact:** The workshop lasts 30 minutes. Source: approved format note, verified 2026-07-30.

## Project state

- **Objective:** Finish the outline for the soil-basics workshop.
- **Status:** In progress.
- **Completed:** Topic and audience were approved in `RECEIPTS.md`.
- **Blocker:** The exercise still needs a low-cost materials check.
- **Next action:** Compare two exercises and choose one that uses common household items.
- **Last updated:** 2026-07-30 by the workshop owner.

## Decision

- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Choice:** Teach soil drainage before fertilizer.
- **Reason:** A drainage check is safer and easier for a beginner to test.
- **Tradeoff:** Fertilizer moves to a later workshop.
- **Evidence:** Approved outline review.
- **Revisit when:** Attendee questions show that fertilizer is blocking the drainage lesson.

## Receipt

- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Action:** Reviewed the outline against the four-part quality standard.
- **Result:** Topic, teaching points, and review owner passed. The exercise remains open.
- **Evidence:** `soil-basics-outline-review.md`
- **State:** Validated
- **Limit:** This receipt does not prove the exercise is affordable.
