# Start Here: Taste and Packaging System

Part of Payton's AI Systems, created and maintained by Payton Bilodeau for AI Mentorship.

Use this system when AI can produce endless drafts, titles, thumbnails, and posts, and the bottleneck has moved to judgment: deciding what is true enough to ship, bold enough to work, and written like a person. It turns your taste from a vague feeling into written rules an AI can actually follow.

## Minimum AI capability

**Chat only** is enough. Every mechanism in this system is a written rule, a gate, or a ledger the AI applies in conversation. No tool, connector, or generation capability is required, although the rules apply to anything those tools later produce.

## Give your AI this message

```text
Read this folder's START HERE.md, SYSTEM.md, every file in templates, and examples/EXAMPLE PACKAGING DECISION.md. Help me lock my own packaging constitution, then act as my quality gate. For every title, hook, thumbnail concept, subject line, or draft I bring you: first filter out anything that fails my honesty floor, then push the survivors to be as compelling as the truth allows, and tell me plainly when I am being timid. Run the writing quality gate on drafts before I ship them, preserve my voice using the voice rules, and record every real packaging decision in my taste ledger. Save approved files under _MY WORK/Taste and Packaging.
```

## Have these ready

- three recent pieces you shipped and how they actually performed;
- one piece you are proud of and one you regret;
- the claims you would never make, even if they would work;
- the audience you write for, described the way you would describe a friend;
- a recent draft or package decision to use as the first live test.

## A good first result

You should end with a locked two-rule constitution in your own words, one draft passed through the quality gate with named fixes, and one real decision recorded in the ledger. The measure of success is a decision that got easier: you knew what to ship and why, faster than before.
