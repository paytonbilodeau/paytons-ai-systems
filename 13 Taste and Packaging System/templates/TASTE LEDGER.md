# Taste Ledger

One row per real decision. The ledger is what turns taste from a mood into a record that can be checked, argued with, and improved.

## Rules for keeping it honest

- Record the decision before shipping, while the reasoning is real rather than reconstructed.
- Write the prediction. A ledger without predictions cannot show you being wrong, and being wrong on record is where taste improves.
- Come back for the actual result once it exists, even when it is embarrassing.
- The lesson column holds one sentence, not an essay. "Bolder framing won again" or "the safe title underperformed as predicted" is enough.

## What belongs here

- title and thumbnail picks;
- hooks and opening lines;
- subject lines;
- ship-or-hold calls on whole pieces;
- any decision where you overrode the AI's recommendation, or it overrode yours.
