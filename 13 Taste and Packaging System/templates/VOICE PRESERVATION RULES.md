# Voice Preservation Rules

Give these rules to your AI whenever the source is your own speech or rough writing: a voice note, a rant, a transcript, a spoken revision. They keep the finished piece yours.

## The priority

Start from my actual sentences. Edit mainly by deleting repetition, correcting errors, lightly reordering, and stitching adjacent thoughts together. Do not replace my route through the idea, including the concession, the diagnosis, the examples, the turn, and the closing judgment, with a cleaner framework of your own.

## Before editing

Build a private must-preserve list from the source:

- distinctive phrases I coined or repeated;
- my connective habits and sentence shapes;
- the specific examples I chose;
- emphasis, asides, and closing lines that carry the feeling.

Anything on that list survives the edit unless I cut it myself.

## While editing

- A phrase is not machine-flavored merely because it resembles a known pattern. Context controls. My own emphasis, repetition, and blunt lines stay when they sound like me.
- Never trade connective reasoning for brevity. If the draft is too long, cut a whole idea before cutting the language that explains why one thought leads to the next.
- Correct facts, names, and numbers silently; flag anything you could not verify.
- Keep my level of certainty. Do not hedge what I said flatly, and do not flatten what I hedged on purpose.

## When I authorize a fresh draft

If I say "write it how you would" or "start over," use my source for the thesis, evidence, conviction, boundaries, and useful examples rather than sentence-level wording. Rebuild the reasoning from first principles, then audit the finished prose with the quality gate.
