## Description draft

When links are the user's priority, lead with them. Give each a short sentence
explaining why someone would visit, then its URL. Put the video summary after
the links. Keep the order configurable; do not invent a creator's offer, pricing
or account destinations. If another order was explicitly chosen, preserve it.

Write a short, specific summary of what the finished video delivers. Use the
topic and tool names naturally. Avoid keyword stuffing, promises absent from
the video and a step-by-step recap that gives away every payoff.

## Chapter draft

Choose a small number of meaningful sections. Around six can suit a long
explainer, but source structure determines the count. Do not add a chapter for
every sub-step. Use concise labels that identify the topic and invite a viewer
to see the answer. "The catch with AI editing" can describe a real limitations
section; "You won't believe this" gives no navigation value.

Use relevant search terms where they help identify the subject, not as a claim
that chapter wording guarantees ranking or retention. Do not reveal every
conclusion in the label or promise a surprise the section does not contain.

- First timestamp is 0:00.
- At least three timestamps, ascending, each chapter at least ten seconds.
- All timestamps refer to the final export and land on meaningful boundaries.
- Use one `0:00 Concise label` line per chapter, without bullets.
- Watch the relevant transitions or use verified final timing before delivery.

Description timestamps create authored chapters and override automatic chapters.
Do not claim that you changed the platform setting or hid chapters just by
writing labels. Verify displayed navigation separately when an upload is
authorized. Current primary guidance: [YouTube chapters](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)
and [description tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en).

## Final check

The title, thumbnail and description should describe the same actual video.
Preserve authentic portraits and evidence; do not invent results. Check the
selected upload route's file limits and the user's tighter delivery target.
Keep full-resolution masters. Deliver exact copy in its own copyable block and
record the final decision in the taste ledger. Publishing remains separate.
