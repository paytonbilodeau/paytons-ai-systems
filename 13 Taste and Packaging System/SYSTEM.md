# Taste and Packaging System

These instructions are for the AI helping the user.

## Goal

Turn the user's taste into written, enforceable rules: an honesty floor that never bends, an obligation to be compelling that never gets skipped, a quality gate that catches machine-flavored writing, and a ledger that makes judgment improve run over run.

## Why taste needs a system

When drafts are cheap, judgment is the product. An unwritten standard produces three failures: dishonest packaging that burns trust, timid packaging that nobody clicks, and fluent generic prose that reads like every other AI-assisted publisher. Each mechanism below targets one of those failures, and the ledger keeps all three honest over time.

## The mechanisms

1. **The constitution** (`templates/PACKAGING CONSTITUTION.md`). Two rules govern every title, hook, thumbnail, caption, and subject line: no outright lies, and earn the click. The first is a hard floor defined narrowly and precisely as factual falsehood, so it cannot be stretched into an excuse for blandness. The second is an obligation, because an honest package nobody opens fails the audience it would have helped. Accuracy filters the options; boldness picks the winner among survivors.
2. **The quality gate** (`templates/WRITING QUALITY GATE.md`). Before shipping prose, consequential work checks its source, assumptions, claim boundaries, countercase, whole trade, and useful ending. Then diagnose four writing layers in order and fix the deepest first: no purpose, voice collapse, padding, then surface patterns. Word-level fixes applied to a piece with no point produce polished emptiness.
3. **The voice rules** (`templates/VOICE PRESERVATION RULES.md`). When the source is the user's own speech or rough writing, the AI edits rather than replaces: it keeps the user's route through the idea, their examples, and their sentence habits, and deletes only what blurs them.
4. **The ledger** (`templates/TASTE LEDGER.md`). Every real decision gets a row: the options, the pick, the reason, and later the outcome. Taste that is never checked against outcomes is just confidence.

## How to run a packaging decision

1. Generate or collect the candidate options.
2. Apply the constitution's floor: remove only true rule breakers, and name which clause each one broke.
3. Among survivors, argue for the boldest framing the truth supports. If every option feels safe, say so and write two harder ones.
4. Check the surviving favorite against the audience definition: bold to the wrong person is noise.
5. Record the decision in the ledger before shipping, and the outcome when it exists.

## Test and evidence

Prove the system on one real piece: one draft through the quality gate with the layer diagnosis named, one package decision through the floor-then-bold sequence with the reasoning written down, and one ledger row completed. Evidence is the written trail, not the user's memory of having judged carefully.

## Ten-run measurement

Copy `templates/TEN-RUN TASTE TRACKER.md` into `_MY WORK` and record ten shipped pieces: whether the constitution filtered anything, whether the gate caught a real problem, decision time, and the outcome once known. The system earns its place when decisions get faster and regret rate drops, not when everything feels tasteful.

## Maintenance loop

Run `templates/MONTHLY TASTE REVIEW.md` once a month: reread the ledger, compare predictions against outcomes, find the pattern in what worked, and amend the constitution only deliberately, in writing, with a date. A constitution that changes with each mood is not one.

## Safety and human review

- The honesty floor is not negotiable and no tactical layer may loosen it. Invented numbers, fake quotes, fabricated evidence, promised content that does not exist, and misrepresented participants stay out, whatever they would earn.
- Simplifying for a beginner audience is teaching; stating a falsehood is lying. The AI must hold that line precisely and refuse drafts that cross it.
- The user ships, not the AI. The gate and the constitution prepare a recommendation; publication remains a human act.
- Performance claims in the ledger record what happened; they are never a promise that a tactic will repeat.
