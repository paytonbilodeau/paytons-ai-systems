# Example Packaging Decision

A filled example. Casey publishes a weekly video explaining AI tools to beginners and needs a title and thumbnail line for an episode showing how a scheduling assistant saved a real four-hour planning session.

## The options

1. "This AI saved me 4 hours in one afternoon"
2. "AI just made planning obsolete"
3. "My honest review of an AI scheduling assistant"
4. "I let an AI plan my whole week. It found 4 hours I was wasting"

## The floor pass

- Option 2 fails clause 1: planning is not obsolete, and the episode does not show that. It promises content the video does not contain. Filtered.
- Options 1, 3, and 4 all pass. The four hours were measured, the test really happened, and nothing is attributed to anyone falsely.

## The boldness pass

- Option 3 passes the floor and fails clause 2. It is technically perfect and completely boring: "honest review" is what a package says when nobody decided what the story is. This is the timidity failure, named out loud.
- Option 1 is true and decent but generic; "saved me time" is the claim every AI video makes.
- Option 4 keeps the measured number, adds the real tension (the wasted hours were already there, unnoticed), and opens a curiosity gap the episode actually pays off in the first minute. It is the boldest framing the truth supports.

## The decision

Option 4, with thumbnail text "4 HOURS. FOUND." over Casey's genuinely surprised face from the real footage. The surprise is allowed exaggeration territory: a bigger expression than the moment, not a fabricated result.

## The ledger row

| Date | Piece | Options considered | Floor filtered | Picked | Why | Predicted result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Tuesday in March | Scheduling assistant episode | 4; strongest were 1 and 4 | Option 2, clause 1, promised content not present | Option 4 | Measured number plus real tension, boldest truthful framing | Above the channel's median views |

Three weeks later Casey recorded the actual result, forty percent above median, and the lesson: "the specific number beat the generic time-saved claim, again."
