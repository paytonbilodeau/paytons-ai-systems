# Filled Example: Choose the First Workshop Format

This fictional example compares a live online workshop with a recorded lesson for a small gardening club.

## Decision brief

- **Decision:** Should the first soil-basics workshop be live or recorded?
- **Desired outcome:** Teach one useful lesson within two weeks and learn what beginners find confusing.
- **Deadline:** Choose by 2026-08-01.
- **Cost of waiting:** The club loses one scheduled teaching week.
- **Hard constraint:** The format must be ready within two weeks.
- **Preference:** The lesson should be reusable.
- **Known fact:** The club already has a meeting link and a 30-minute time slot. Source: approved event note.
- **Estimate:** Editing a recorded lesson may take four to six hours.
- **Unknown:** How many questions beginners will ask.
- **Reversibility:** A live workshop can be followed by a recording later. A polished recording takes more work before any feedback arrives.
- **Decision standard:** Launch within two weeks and collect at least five useful questions.

## Option comparison

| Criterion | Live workshop | Recorded lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Meets two-week limit | Yes | Probably |
| Time to useful result | One scheduled session | Four to six estimated editing hours |
| Feedback quality | Questions arrive during the session | Feedback arrives after publishing |
| Reuse | Outline can be reused | Finished video can be reused |
| Reversibility | Easy to repeat or record later | Editing time is already spent |

## Decision

- **Choice:** Run the live workshop first.
- **Reason:** It meets the deadline and answers the biggest unknown faster.
- **Tradeoff:** There is no polished reusable video in the first two weeks.
- **Confidence:** Medium. The attendance count is still unknown.
- **Revisit when:** The live session produces fewer than five useful questions or the club asks for an on-demand version.

## Action plan

- **Next action:** Draft the 30-minute live outline.
- **Owner:** Workshop owner.
- **Due:** 2026-08-03.
- **Definition of done:** The outline contains three teaching points, one exercise, and five minutes for questions.
- **Approval needed:** The club organizer confirms the date.
- **Receipt:** Approved outline saved in the working folder.
