## Handoff after a subscription change

Before retiring a tool, identify the recurring jobs, fallback routes, update checks, and review steps that still call it. Assign each required job to an available tool and verify one safe run; preserve source files and useful handoffs until the replacement has proven access.

- **Affected jobs and new owners:** [job, owner, verification receipt]
- **Retired routes:** [scheduled calls and automatic fallbacks removed or disabled]
- **Preserved context:** [canonical files, unfinished work, retained compatibility copies]
- **Replacement access and billing:** [verified file access, authentication route, shared usage limits, separately charged capabilities]
- **Unavailable fallback:** [checkpoint location and when work resumes]

A remaining subscription does not establish that an API, connector, or automation route is included. Verify the chosen route and its billing before making it a fallback.
