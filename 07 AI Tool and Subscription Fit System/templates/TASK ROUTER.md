Use the user's current tool when it meets the requirement. A new specialist needs a repeated measured reason.

## Routing guidance

- Keep creative decisions, ambiguous work, and final review with a capable model and one primary owner.
- Use a cheaper bounded worker for an independent support job with a clear acceptance check; escalate its evidence after a failed check.
- Run a qualified deterministic script first for known transformations, measurements, metadata, rendering, or verification.
- A compact handoff names: objective, exact inputs, owned outputs, constraints, acceptance check, and escalation condition.
- Do not create recursive supervisors or permanent watchers. Keep one owner at decisions and final review.

Give a worker fresh, relevant context rather than the entire conversation. Complete small jobs directly when handoff and review overhead would exceed the benefit. Keep raw logs in files and return concise evidence.
