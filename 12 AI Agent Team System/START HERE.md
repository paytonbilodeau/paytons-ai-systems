# Start Here: AI Agent Team System

Part of Payton's AI Systems, created and maintained by Payton Bilodeau.

Use this system when you want a small team of AI agents on a hosted bot platform: one front-door agent you talk to, plus a few functional specialists it routes work to, connected carefully to your real accounts and proven trustworthy one test at a time.

## Minimum AI capability

**Chat only** is enough, because the whole system is built from written role descriptions, rules, and tests you paste into the platform. **Connected app** access is what the platform itself adds once you wire connectors, and every connection follows the checklist in this folder. **Current official information** is required before claiming what a specific platform can do, because hosted bot products change features, limits, and pricing quickly. The AI must check current official documentation instead of answering from memory.

## Give your AI this message

```text
Read this folder's START HERE.md, SYSTEM.md, THIRD-PARTY.md, every file in templates, and examples/EXAMPLE TEAM PILOT.md. Help me set up a small agent team on the hosted bot platform I name: one front-door agent plus Research, Writing, and Operations specialists. Fill the bootstrap prompt and role descriptions from the templates, enter the require-approval rules before any allow rules, and connect one account at a time with the connector checklist, read access proven before write access. Do not let me skip an acceptance test. Never ask me to paste a password, token, or two-factor code into chat. Save approved files under _MY WORK/Agent Team.
```

## Have these ready

- the hosted bot platform you are trying, its plan, and its monthly price;
- the accounts you might connect, and which of them touch clients, money, or publishing;
- two or three recurring outcomes you want the team to own;
- where your real files live, and which folder the platform may write into;
- the actions that must always wait for your approval;
- a end date for the pilot and what would make you keep paying.

## A good first result

You should end with a named front-door agent, three specialist descriptions pasted in, approval rules active, one connector proven read-only, and the first three acceptance tests passed. A finished connector list is not the result; a verified, bounded working team is.
