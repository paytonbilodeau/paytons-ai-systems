For a tutorial, verify the relevant current official API or tool behavior before
implementing. Record what was already covered, adopted, experimental or rejected.
For human corrections, record the actual source change and its reason instead
of copying a global silence threshold or targeting a percentage removed.

Keep reusable mechanisms separate from this speaker's taste, private assets,
client-specific rules and reference rights. Preserve working components when a
single property needs repair. After an accepted change, update the relevant
skill and test it on another sentence or source shot. Keep the old version.

Review after ten runs, an important failure, a tool update or a scope change.
Do not claim a new technique improves every edit after one favorable example.
