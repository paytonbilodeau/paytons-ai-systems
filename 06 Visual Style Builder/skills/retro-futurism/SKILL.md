# Retro-Futurism

Generate an image in the locked retro-futurism style. The style stays constant; only the metaphor, palette weighting, and mood change per image.

## Before generating

1. Turn the topic into one clear visual metaphor. One. If two metaphors are competing, make the user pick.
2. Choose a limited palette with strong warm-cool contrast, and decide which side dominates: warmer and lighter for tutorials and wins, cooler and darker for warnings and commentary.
3. If the image involves a real company or product, get the current official logo asset and supply it to the generator as a visual reference. Never redraw or retype a real mark.
4. If the image carries text, fix the exact words now, keep them short, and forbid any extra text in the prompt.

## Prompt structure

Build the prompt in this order: the metaphor as a scene, then style law, then palette, then text and logo instructions.

Style law to include every time: mid-century retro-futurism illustration; hard-edged geometric color blocks; dark outlines; flat fills with clear boundaries, no smooth gradients; light vintage print texture in restrained areas only; readable at thumbnail size.

## Hard rules

- One metaphor per image.
- Flat fills and clear shape boundaries instead of gradients or smooth modern CGI.
- Print texture is an accent, never a blanket over the whole image.
- Any supplied official logo is integrated into an object or surface in the scene, exactly once, never pasted like an app tile in a corner.
- Everything, including all words and marks, generates in one coherent pass so lighting and perspective belong to the scene.

## Never

- photorealism or smooth 3D rendering;
- rainbow palettes;
- literal software screenshots;
- several competing metaphors;
- a pasted-looking logo badge;
- repairing text or logos afterward with an overlay. A misspelled or distorted word means a targeted edit or a regeneration.

## QA before delivering

Inspect at full size and at phone size: exact spelling of every word, one logo only and it matches the official mark, the metaphor reads in under a second, edges are clean, and nothing looks added after the fact. Reject and regenerate rather than explain a flaw away.
