# Black-and-White Ink Starter Card

This example is based on Payton's Black-and-White Ink system.

## Definition

A conceptual black ink sketch on a pure white background, using confident hand-drawn linework, varied line weight, selective hatching, and intentional white space.

## Essential rules

- Use black ink and a flat white background.
- Let line weight separate the focal subject from supporting detail.
- Use hatching only where it clarifies form or depth.
- Keep the composition bold enough to read small.
- Center the main idea inside a safe crop area.
- Prefer one symbolic action or contrast.

## Avoid

- color;
- paper grain or notebook texture;
- gray washes;
- borders and frames;
- decorative detail with no role;
- long text.

## Test

Render one close-up action and one wide symbolic scene. Both should feel like the same ink language even though the scale changes.
