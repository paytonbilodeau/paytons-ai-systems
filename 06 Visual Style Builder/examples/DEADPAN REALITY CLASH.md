# Deadpan Reality Clash Starter Card

This example is based on Payton's Deadpan Reality Clash system.

## Definition

Flat two-dimensional cartoon people sit against a detailed realistic environment. The visual separation is deliberate, and the expressions stay grounded rather than theatrical.

## Essential rules

- Use flat solid foreground colors and thin uniform black outlines.
- Keep cartoon people free of shading, gradients, texture, highlights, and cast shadows.
- Make the background detailed, realistic, and materially specific.
- Keep the two layers visibly separate.
- Match expression to the idea with restrained body language.
- Use one scene that reads quickly.

## Avoid

- blending the character into the background light;
- three-dimensional cartoon rendering;
- anime reactions;
- painterly foreground texture;
- global filters that unify both layers;
- text that explains the whole concept.

## Test

Place one calm cartoon character in two different realistic settings. The character system should stay identical while the background light and materials change.
