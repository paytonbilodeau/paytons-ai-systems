## Style specification

- **Name:** Paper Garden Notes
- **Definition:** A simple educational sketch built from black pencil lines, warm cream paper shapes, and one muted green accent.
- **Essential rules:**
  1. Use one large object or action as the focal point.
  2. Keep the background plain and warm.
  3. Use green only to direct attention.
- **Composition:** Place the focal object slightly off center with open space for one short label.
- **Rendering:** Uneven pencil lines, flat paper shapes, no realistic shadows, and no glossy finish.
- **People:** Use simple hands only when an action needs them. Avoid detailed faces.
- **Typography:** Use no more than four hand-lettered words.
- **Logos:** Omit logos unless the user supplies an approved official asset.
- **Never do:** Photorealism, several competing objects, bright rainbow color, or long text.

## Image brief

- **Purpose:** Explain that overwatering can harm a seedling.
- **Core idea:** A large watering can pours toward a tiny plant while a green stop mark sits between them.
- **Ratio:** 4:5 vertical.
- **Focal point:** The gap between the water and the plant.
- **Text:** `Pause before pouring`
- **Avoid:** A sad human face, realistic soil texture, or a branded watering can.
- **Review standard:** The idea reads without the text, the green accent appears once, and the plant remains clear in a square crop.
